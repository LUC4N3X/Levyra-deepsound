# Features

Levyra brings together native media playback, discovery feeds, local downloads, and private listening tools in a single player.

## Playback

- Native Media3 and ExoPlayer pipeline on Android.
- Standalone libvlc playback engine on Windows.
- Full queue management with shuffle, repeat, and custom Queue Spaces.
- Gapless playback and adjustable crossfade.
- ReplayGain and loudness normalization.
- Playback speed and pitch adjustments.
- Built-in sleep timer.
- Android Auto integration and media notification controls.
- Seamless switching between audio-only and native video streams.

## Discovery

- Your Orbit feed for quick access to your regular rotation.
- Quick Picks tailored to recent listening habits.
- International top charts and genre collections.
- Personalized radio stations based on songs or artists.
- Similar song recommendations.
- Rediscovery shelves highlighting older favorites.
- Dedicated artist discographies and album pages.
- Vertical video clips and short-form preview feeds where available.

## Lyrics and visuals

- Time-synchronized lyrics with tap-to-seek navigation.
- Manual offset controls to fix early or late lyric timing.
- Bluetooth-specific lyric timing offsets.
- Multi-script romanization for non-Latin writing systems.
- Shareable lyric cards in square and story formats.
- Full-screen Canvas video loops with motion artwork fallbacks.
- Levyra Ambient mode for a subdued, OLED-friendly now-playing display.

!!! note
    Visual enhancements are decorative. Album artwork, Canvas videos, and animations never delay audible playback.

## Downloads and offline listening

Downloaded tracks are saved as standard M4A files directly to your storage with embedded metadata:

- Track title, artist, and album tags
- High-resolution embedded artwork
- Synchronized or plain-text lyrics

Because these are standard media files, you can play them in any third-party media player or move them between devices.

## Library management

- Favorites, custom playlists, and playlist tags.
- Filter options and the ability to hide selected playlists.
- Followed artist feeds and artist exclusions for recommendations.
- On-device listening history and weekly activity statistics.
- Full backup and restore via Levyra Vault without requiring a cloud service.

## Music identification and tools

- Song recognition via microphone input.
- Internal audio capture recognition on supported Android versions.
- Optional scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
- SponsorBlock integration to skip intros, outros, and non-music segments.
- Custom network controls, including DNS-over-HTTPS and HTTP proxy settings.

## Privacy

Using Levyra does not require:

- Creating an account
- Building an advertising profile
- Sending usage telemetry or analytics
- Syncing personal data to external servers

Read [Privacy & Data](privacy.md) for full technical details.
