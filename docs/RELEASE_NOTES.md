# Levyra 3.2.1

## Highlights

You can now bring saved photo and video posts into Levyra and play them offline. The Default skin also gets a quieter look across the Library, Explore and Radio, with another pass on Search and the Home soundtrack card.

Downloads are easier to follow when you have a long queue. Artist pages show their first songs sooner, and the artists you pick during setup can shape your recommendations before you play anything.

## ✦ Saved posts, with their music

Open Posts in the Library and choose a folder or ZIP from your device. Levyra can import a set of photos with its music and caption, or a saved video. Once imported, the post is available to replay offline from the Library.

## ✦ A clearer download queue

The download card shows the current track, overall progress and how many tracks are saved or still queued. Tap the card to open Downloads. Its close button now lets you cancel the whole queue after confirmation, including tracks from a selection that was still being queued.

Artist menus show download progress for the full catalog. Popular tracks that are already saved are marked in the list, so you can see what is ready to play offline.

## ✦ A calmer Default skin

Explore, Library and Radio soften their title bars as you scroll. The Radio screen gives the station that is on air a more open layout, and My Soundtrack blends the artist art into its card. Search tones down the suggestion chips, and Listening Pulse looks at the last seven days.

Artist pages can show the profile and first page of songs while the rest loads. The artists you choose in onboarding can also seed Daily Discover and suggestions on a fresh install.

## Validation

The Android publishing workflow checks the version fields, runs release lint and builds the signed APK. It verifies the APK signature and generates a SHA-256 checksum before publication.

## Upgrade notes

Install the update through the same channel you used for your current copy. The new Posts section uses files you select through Import folder or Import ZIP.

## Final note

If a saved post imports without its music or the download card shows the wrong count, include the file type or the steps you took when you open an issue.
