# Levyra 3.2.5

## Highlights

This update is mostly about the player. The Player Deck has three new players, you can paste your own synced lyrics, video mode finds the official music video, and volume normalization no longer changes the level halfway through a song.

## New

- Turntable player: the cover spins on a record under a tonearm that follows the song, and you drag around the platter to jump to any point. It takes the place of Gallery
- Cinema player: synced lyrics fill the screen over a blurred backdrop lit by the cover. Songs without lyrics show the cover with a small visualizer. It takes the place of Sleeve
- Stack player: your queue as a pile of cards. Drag the front card to skip, lift the pile to open the queue. It takes the place of Liner Notes
- Your own lyrics: paste LRC text from the lyrics options and it plays in sync, beats every online source and stays after a restart. Restore original lyrics brings the online ones back
- Video options sheet: Picture in Picture, quality, subtitles and the list of matching videos in one place. The video you pick is remembered for that song
- News reader: stories in Explore open inside Levyra with the photo, author, reading time and the full article
- Mi Play on Xiaomi and HyperOS: the audio output button opens Mi Play on phones that have it
- Your Last.fm playlists in Settings > Integrations, with loved and top tracks, each one ready to import
- A Volume normalization switch in Settings > Audio > Dynamics

## Improved

- Volume normalization: one fixed level per song, so quiet intros and loud choruses keep the dynamics they were recorded with. A song without loudness data is measured during its first 30 seconds and gets its level from the next play
- Video mode picks the official music video over lyric videos, fan uploads, covers and remixes, and moves to the next one if a video won't play
- Player Deck: swipe through phone-sized previews of every player and tap the one you want. Swiping no longer switches the player by itself
- Opening the player in the Default skin: the mini player rises with it and the bottom tabs slide away, and both come back when you close it
- Apple-style player: Google Cast moved from the header into the three-dot menu
- Lossless audio: Arcod is back as the last FLAC source, Tidal lookups start sooner, and songs credited with "(con ...)" stay in high quality
- Update screen: a new Levyra banner, the main changes in your language, and Later only hides it until the next launch
- Startup on 4 GB phones does less background work, and Home scrolls with less memory and GPU load
- Trending artists always show monthly listeners

## Fixed

- Offline artists, playlists and mixes in Library that wouldn't open, and downloads deleted outside Levyra that stayed in the Offline list
- Last.fm looking disconnected after coming back from the browser, and Audio efficiency showing up untranslated
- The Your daily discover shelf going missing for new users
- The front cover in Stack flashing blank or doubled on a track change

## Upgrade notes

Install the update from the same channel you use now, such as GitHub or F-Droid. If you had picked Gallery, Sleeve or Liner Notes in the Player Deck, Levyra switches you to Turntable, Cinema or Stack. You can turn volume normalization off in Settings > Audio > Dynamics.
