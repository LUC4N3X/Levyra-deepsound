Levyra 2.3.20 fixes external playback controls. Hardware media keys, remotes, the lock screen, the system media panel and other media controllers can now skip to the next or previous track, which previously worked only inside the app. Skip requests go through the existing queue, so radio, repeat, shuffle and history behave as usual. The shuffle and favorite media-control labels now come from the string catalog instead of being hardcoded to Italian. Libraries, favorites, playlists, downloads and settings are unchanged.
